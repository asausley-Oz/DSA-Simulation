# Capacity and Correspondence
### How the Infinite God and the Finite Creature Genuinely Meet

---

## The thesis in one line

Correspondence is the real relation between God and the creature. Capacity is the creaturely measure of how much of that relation the creature can presently hold. Accommodation is correspondence scaled to capacity — the infinite God meeting the finite creature at the level the creature can receive.

The three are one motion seen from three angles. There is no correspondence *with a creature* that is not accommodated, because the creature is finite. There is no accommodation that is not correspondence, because what is scaled down is a genuine relation and not a substitute for one. And capacity is simply the name for the receiving end of the whole transaction.

---

## Why capacity exists

Capacity is not a brute fact about creatures. It is the trace of a single distinction that nothing in salvation ever erases: the divide between the uncreated God and the created creature.

Theosis — the creature's transformation into the likeness of God, its drawing-in to the divine life — dissolves nearly everything. It dissolves the distance of estrangement, the barrier of sin, the unlikeness between the creature and its Lord. The one thing it does not dissolve, and must not, is the line between Creator and creature. The creature, however deified, remains creature: finite, created, receiving its being from another.

Because the creature remains finite, it has a capacity — a measure of how much it can hold. An infinite being has no such measure; it is scaled to nothing. So capacity is the permanent signature of the creature/creator divide, written into the creature's very mode of receiving God. Where there is capacity, there is a creature; where there is a creature, there is the divide; and the divide is the floor beneath the whole structure.

---

## Accommodation: correspondence scaled to capacity

Because God is love, He does not stand at the far edge of the divide and wait for the creature to climb. Love meets the beloved where the beloved is. So God comes down to the creature's capacity and corresponds with it there. That is accommodation.

It is not a lesser gift or a watered-down God. It is the real God, given in the measure the creature can bear. Accommodation is simply what love does across a divide it has no intention of erasing.

Three things follow.

**It is universal.** No creature receives God raw. The angels no more comprehend the divine essence than humans do; the heavens are themselves accommodated environments, places where God restricts the full weight of His presence to what the inhabitants can sustain. Anything any creature comprehends of God is already accommodation. The gap accommodation spans is never earth-below and heaven-above — it is Creator-and-creature, everywhere.

**It has a location.** "Where two or three gather in my name, there am I in their midst." The midst — the relational space between gathered persons — is a space God fills. He does not collapse it; the two or three remain two or three. He inhabits it. Presence, not absorption.

**It reaches its summit in the Incarnation.** The hypostatic union is the supreme accommodation: God the Son meeting the human creature at the creature's own capacity by taking the creature's nature. And it is accommodation without loss — two natures fully united without confusion. Christ is the place where accommodation and union-without-assimilation are one event.

---

## Growing capacity: theosis and eternal increase

Capacity is not a fixed ceiling. The creature is being enlarged — from glory to glory, made a partaker of the divine nature, grown toward the likeness of God. As capacity grows, accommodation grows with it: the more the creature can hold, the more of Himself God gives.

This means the relation never reaches a static end. The creature, forever finite, is forever enlarged toward a God who is infinite — so the receiving never stops deepening. It never exhausts God, because He is infinite and the creature is not. It never arrives, because arriving would mean becoming infinite, which is to stop being a creature. It always increases. This is eternal increase — what the tradition calls *epektasis*, the endless advance into God.

So "the accommodation never lifts" is not a deprivation. It is the engine of an inexhaustible joy. The horizon keeps receding precisely because the creature keeps growing toward it. The gap that looks like a limit is the very thing that makes the increase eternal.

---

## The two gaps: distance and the divide

Two gaps are easy to confuse, and the difference is load-bearing.

**Distance** is the gap of estrangement — the relational rupture that accumulates as covenants are broken, the curse-vector of the Distance Cycle. Distance is a wound, and theosis closes it completely. That is the whole point of the redemptive movement: the distance between God and humanity is abolished in Christ.

**The creature/creator divide** is not distance. It is the good gap — the deepest form of space, the ontological room that keeps the creature a distinct other rather than a part of God. Theosis does not close this gap; it preserves it, precisely so the union stays communion and does not become absorption.

So the redemptive movement closes all the distance and keeps all the space. Capacity is the name of that kept space, measured from the creature's side. To lose it would not be a deeper union; it would be the end of the two-ness that any union requires.

---

## Why it is communion and not absorption

Beneath all of this is the root: God is an eternal community of love, and correspondence is relational — it requires persons. Relation needs two. Love needs a someone and an Other.

This is why the creature/creator divide is not a limit to be mourned but the condition of the relationship. If theosis dissolved the divide — if the creature received God unaccommodated and became infinite — there would be no creature left to commune, no other to be loved, no two-ness to hold the relation open. Erasing the divide does not yield a closer union; it yields the drop returning to the ocean, the impersonal One, with no one left at the meeting.

The same principle runs creature to creature: the communion of saints remains many persons, not melted into one. Personhood is preserved all the way up and all the way down, because the source is itself persons in communion. Capacity, accommodation, and the undissolved divide are the means by which a love that could have collapsed into oneness instead remains a love among distinct persons forever.

This is "complete union without assimilation" stated in the register of being: theosis dissolving everything else is the *complete union*; the creature/creator divide it cannot dissolve is the *without assimilation*.

---

## Christ, where it meets

Every thread gathers in the Incarnation. Christ is the source of correspondence — the eternal Word in whom the relation between divine intent and created reality is grounded. He is the supreme accommodation — God meeting the creature at the creature's capacity by taking the creature's nature. And He is the pattern of union-without-assimilation — two natures fully one without confusion, the creature's nature carried into the divine life without ceasing to be the creature's nature.

What is true of the Head becomes the trajectory of the body: drawn into genuine union with God, accommodated at every step to a growing capacity, deified by grace, and never — not at the end, not in glory, not for an archangel — ceasing to be the creature God loves across the one divide He keeps.

---

## Load-bearing commitments

The account stands on these:

1. **The creature/creator divide is inviolable.** It is the one thing theosis does not dissolve, the floor beneath capacity, and the guarantee that union remains communion rather than absorption. Deified by grace, never by nature.

2. **Accommodation is universal and permanent.** Every creature in every realm receives an accommodated God; no created vantage, not even the heavenly, ever stands before God unaccommodated.

3. **Capacity grows; the end is eternal increase.** The account commits to *epektasis* — endless advance into God — rather than a fixed beatific rest. The creature forever receives more and never exhausts the infinite.

4. **The resolution is relational, not mechanical.** Capacity and accommodation answer *how* the eternal God meets the temporal, finite creature by way of love and presence, not by a metaphysical mechanism. The puzzles of foreknowledge and divine timelessness are dissolved by reframing knowledge as interpersonal and presence as the filling of the midst — an answer given in the framework's own register, by design.

5. **Correspondence requires personhood.** The whole structure rests on the relational source — God as an eternal community of love — for whom relation, and therefore the preservation of distinct persons, is first.

---

*Capacity and correspondence are finally one subject: how a finite creature genuinely holds an infinite God. Correspondence is the relation; capacity is what the creature can hold of it; accommodation is love scaling the one to the other; and the creature/creator divide is the reason the scaling never ends — and never needs to.*
